# Code of Conduct

Make learning welcoming across nations, languages, abilities, and experience
levels. Give specific, respectful feedback about work. Explain mistakes without
insults, pressure, or promises about employment or income.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and sharing
another person's private information are unacceptable. Respect reference licenses
and a learner's choice not to share activity or identity.

Report concerns to the repository owner through an agreed private channel; do not
post private evidence publicly. A reporting contact and human moderation/appeal
process must be established before community participation opens. An LLM may not
independently impose irreversible sanctions.
